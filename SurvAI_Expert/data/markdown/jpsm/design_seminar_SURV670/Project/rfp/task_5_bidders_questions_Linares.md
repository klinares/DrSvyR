# Bidders Conference Questions

CTtransit Onboard Customer Satisfaction Surveys RFP

*Submitted March 17, 2026*

**Dead Reckoning Survey Solutions,** is a minority-veteran-owned survey research firm and an Equal Opportunity Employer. We specialize in marketing research, including evaluation research for clients such as Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority, to assess customer satisfaction and product performance. The following questions are submitted on behalf of our expert team to clarify several aspects of the RFP before we finalize our proposal.

**1.** What ridership data, such as average daily boardings by route or by service area, can CTDOT provide to help bidders develop a proportional sampling plan across the 183 routes?

**2.** What level of statistical precision does CTDOT expect from the survey results; for example, a specific margin of error at the service area level, or at the route level?

**3.** How does CTDOT envision the ten service areas being distributed across the three-year contract period (i.e., all areas each year)?

**4.** What operational data sources, such as automatic vehicle location or automatic passenger counters, does CTtransit currently maintain, and under what conditions could the Contractor access them for analytical purposes?

**5.** What types of respondent incentives and what per-respondent value range would CTDOT consider appropriate for increasing participation among low-income and underrepresented riders?

**6.** To what extent would CTDOT be open to piloting a smartphone-based rider panel, where volunteers use an app that tracks their trips and asks a few quick questions after each ride, to supplement the onboard survey with real-time travel data?

**7.** What Wi-Fi and cellular connectivity conditions should the Contractor plan for when designing the tablet and QR code survey experience onboard CTtransit vehicles?

Thank you for the opportunity to ask these questions. I look forward to the discussion on Thursday.
