* Types of consulting
    * Why engage a consultant, because the problem usually evolves the most cost effective solution. Reduces amount of time to get to the solution, internal cost, and involves the acquisition of someone’s expertise.
    * Successful consulting
        * establish rapport -interpersonal, to converse easily and ask questions
        * identify the problem in agreement with the client, identify early, come to an agreement with the client.
        * Set team goals to providing solution and assign tasks to team members depending on skills sets.
        * Agree on responsibilities with team
        * evaluate results
        * present the product
* statistical consulting in academia
