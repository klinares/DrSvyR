### Datassist Interview Questions

**Question 1.** What are the top three types of statistical methods or techniques you enjoy using?

**Question 2.** What do you like best about working with data?

**Question 3.** In a multiple regression of Y on three predictors, X1, X2 and X3, if the coefficients of both X2 and X3, are not significant, is it safe to drop these two variable and perform a regression on X1 alone? Discuss in a way a client would understand.

**Question 4.** In a regression model with two predictors X1 and X2, and an interaction term between the two predictors, how do you interpret the \`main' effects of X1 and X2 when the model includes an interaction term that is significant? Discuss in a way a non---technical client would understand.

**Question 5:** If you were asked to do an analysis that was meant to be through a "gender lens" and the covariate for gender was not significant what would you do?

# Organizing & Writing a Proposal: Assurances

**BUDGET**

A budget detailing how the requested funds will be used must accompany all grant requests.

General Support Requests: the organization’s **institutional budget** will suffice; an additional budget is not required.

**REQUIRED Supporting Materials**

<u>All Applicants</u>

* Copy of the organization’s **current institutional budget**
* **Sources of institutional support** (if not mentioned in the above), including pending requests
* List of **Board members**, including their affiliations
* **Diversity Form** (link can be found here: <u>[http://www.rbf.org/content/apply-grant](http://www.rbf.org/content/apply-grant)</u>)

<u>First-time applicants</u> also have to submit documentation of their tax status and a copy of their most recent audited financial statements; your program contact will advise you on what is required.

**Format**

There is no minimum or maximum length requirement for the proposal; however, the RBF has found that 3 to 10 pages is an adequate range. Please be concise.

**Submission of Materials**

The RBF strongly prefers to receive electronic versions of proposal materials. These should be e-mailed directly to your program contact at the Fund. Electronic submission of proposal materials, however, is not a requirement, and you will not be penalized if you do not have access to e-mail. If you are unable to send the materials electronically, please send hard copies. **Please do not send both electronic and hard copies of your proposal materials.**

**Additional Materials**

Materials such as press coverage, brochures, annual reports, DVDs, or CD-ROMs are not required for review of the proposal and will be requested by RBF program staff if needed.

<u>[https://www.rbf.org/sites/default/files/WhattoIncludeProposal_revised%20December%202012.pdf](https://www.rbf.org/sites/default/files/WhattoIncludeProposal_revised%20December%202012.pdf)</u>

# Example 1: Annotated Bibliography

---

A substantive compilation of what we know about the problem from prior research:
- Types of models or theories used
- Main concepts employed

A review of important methodological issues in prior research (annotated and with comments):
- Examples of items and questionnaires
  - Form, scales, order, etc.
- Issues of selection of units
  - Frames (available or not), sample designs
