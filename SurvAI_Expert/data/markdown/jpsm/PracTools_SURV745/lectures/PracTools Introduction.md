# What is this class about? - 2

1. Single-stage surveys — sample size calculations (CV targets, power), different types of designs ($srs$, $stsrs$, $pps$)
2. Math programming
3. Adjustments for sample losses
4. Multistage samples — 2- and 3-stage designs, variance components, allocations to stages
5. Area Samples — Census geographic units, composite MOS
6. Steps in weighting — base weights, adjustments for unknown nonresponse, use of auxiliary data
7. Calibration — poststratification, raking, GREG, weight variability
8. Weighting adjustment approaches for nonprobability sampling
9. Sample size calculation
   * Estimate precision
   * Statistical power for hypothesis testing

3. What are the types of surveys your clients will use?
4. What are the types of sampling your clients will use?
