TSE II

1/13/26

* Concepts: What causes, how to measure, how to reduce error.
* What do we want to accomplish in the course? Experience with a problem, not sampling error, and conduct an analysis.
  * Why does it matter, what caused it, how can we measure it, and we propose how to reduce it. At the end, output a methodological scientific research. The research idea and questions is more important. We will understand source of error. We will also learn to receive and give feedback.
  * Group presentation – 4 papers assigned, and we will breakdown how are they structured and discuss the methodological gap addressed.
  * Scientific reasoning – think about the target population, constructs.
  * Things to consider with research question, has to be relevant, linked to error source, ways to measure, reduce, and evaluate. It has to be manageable and original. Read the relevant literature.
  * Parts of a paper
    * Abstract, see example in lecture 1. It has sections: Background, objective, methods, results, conclusions, and contributions.
    * Introduction of a paper, the motivation of why we should care followed by literature review and where is the gap. A hypothesis may also take the form of a research question.
    * Methods, how the research was carried out and what was done.
    * Results, follow good standards for figures and tables. Don’t distract reader, interpret the results that are relevant and move on.
    * Discussion, the so what and what do the results mean.
    * Remember, tell a story.
