# SurvAI Expert

**A survey methodologist to consult whenever you produce a survey estimate.**

Every number that comes from a survey differs from the truth for more than
one reason: who could be reached, who was selected, who answered, how the
questions were asked, and how the answers were handled. The Total Survey
Error framework names these sources of error so that each can be checked
instead of overlooked. This tool is built around it.

## How it was built

Teaching material from graduate training in survey methodology was turned
into a library, and every passage, formula and code example in it was
catalogued by the source of error and the survey step it concerns. When you
ask a question, the tool searches that library and writes its answer from
what it finds. When you describe a situation, it lays out your options, what
each does to the sources of error, and what it would do, and it marks its own
judgment as such. Formulas are shown exactly as they appear in the library.
The Methodology tab explains this and how the tool was tested.

It does not see or run your data. You make the decisions: for those that
carry weight, check its advice with a methodologist.
